# Audience — SMART Repairs

## Location
- **Core:** Warwickshire + surrounding service areas.
- **Practical setup:** a radius around your base covering the service area — e.g. **Rugby + ~20–25 miles**, which captures Rugby, Leamington Spa, Warwick, Kenilworth, Coventry, Southam, Dunchurch, Daventry, Lutterworth, and reaches toward Solihull / Stratford / Balsall Common.
- Alternatively pin the **key Warwickshire towns** individually if you want tighter control.
- **Delivery setting:** "People living in or recently in this location" (not "travelling through") — you want local car owners.
- Do **not** target nationally or beyond practical mobile range.

## Age
- **24–65** (as specified). This spans younger car owners through to established professionals/families — all realistic SMART-repair customers.

## Gender
- **All.**

## Detailed targeting
- **Recommendation: keep it broad / Advantage+ Audience.** SMART repair demand is broad — anyone who's scuffed a bumper or scratched a door is a prospect, regardless of interests. Broad + strong creative lets Meta's algorithm find the people about to need you.
- If you prefer a defined audience as a *suggestion* (Meta can still expand), layer light interests: car ownership, specific marques (BMW, Audi, Mercedes, Range Rover, Tesla, VW), motoring, new-car buyers, car finance/leasing. But **broad is the recommended default** for this objective.
- **Advantage+ Audience:** ON — provide age/location as a signal, let Meta optimise.

## Exclusions
- None needed at launch. (Optionally exclude existing customers later if you build a customer list, to focus spend on new enquiries.)

## Why this works
SMART repair is a **distress/opportunity purchase** — the trigger is damage, not an interest. So the winning lever is **reach × creative**, not narrow targeting. Broad audience + premium before/after imagery + a frictionless WhatsApp CTA is the highest-performing structure for this service.
