# Campaign Settings — SMART Repairs

## Campaign level
- **Name:** `BH | Meta Messages | SMART Repairs | Warwickshire | 2026`
- **Objective:** Engagement (Messages)
- **Buying type:** Auction
- **Budget:** Campaign or ad-set level daily budget. With one ad set, **ad-set daily budget** is simplest and gives cleanest control for scaling. (If you later add more ad sets, switch to Advantage Campaign Budget / CBO.)
- **Starting budget:** £5/day (per your plan). *Recommendation: £10/day if budget allows — a Messages campaign learns faster with more daily conversations; £5/day is fine but slower to exit learning.*
- **Bid strategy:** Highest volume (lowest cost), no cost cap to start.
- **Special ad category:** None.

## Optimisation & delivery
- Optimise for **conversations**, not link clicks — this tells Meta to find people who actually **message**, which is your real goal (photos in the inbox).
- Attribution for messaging is conversation-based; leave Meta's default messaging attribution.
- **Advantage+ creative enhancements:** keep image enhancements **OFF** or minimal — this is a **premium** brand and auto-filters/overlays cheapen the look. Standard enhancements (brightness) are fine; avoid anything that adds stickers/frames.

## Tracking
- Messaging conversations are tracked natively by Meta (conversations started, cost per conversation) — no pixel/landing page needed for this objective.
- **Manual lead quality tracking (important):** in WhatsApp, tag each enquiry: *photo received / quoted / booked / out of area / not proceeding*. Optimise the campaign by **booked jobs**, not just conversation volume. Feed this back weekly.

## Do-not list (brand compliance)
- No prices in ads (indicative-only rule — quote after photos).
- No "invisible repair", "guaranteed removal", or "every scratch fixable" — assessment-based language only.
- No discounts, no countdowns, no fake urgency, no gimmicks.
- British English throughout. Keep it premium.
