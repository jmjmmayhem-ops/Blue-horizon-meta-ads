# Creative Guide — SMART Repairs

The creative must **not** look like an advert. It should look like a premium detailer's own work, quietly confident.

## Do
- **Premium before/after imagery** as the hero — the transformation is the ad.
- Clean, modern, uncluttered. Lots of the car, little else.
- Consistent lighting and angle between "before" and "after" so the improvement is honest and obvious.
- Higher-end / desirable vehicles where possible (they stop the scroll and signal quality) — but any clean, well-shot repair works.
- If you use a label, keep it minimal: a small "Before" / "After" tag, tasteful, in brand colours.
- Sharp focus on the repaired area; a wider "hero" shot of the car can work for the after.

## Don't
- No loud graphics, no clutter, no busy collages.
- No discount banners, no "SALE", no prices.
- No fake urgency ("today only", "hurry").
- No heavy text overlays — keep on-image text minimal (well under 20% of the image). Let the copy do the talking.
- No stock-photo look, no cheesy filters/stickers, no auto-enhancements that cheapen it.

## Before/after formats (both work)
- **Split (side-by-side):** before left, after right — instantly readable.
- **Slider/stack:** before top, after bottom — good for vertical (4:5 / 9:16).
- Keep the split line clean and the two halves the same scale/angle.

## Feel
Premium. Professional. Calm. Trustworthy. The viewer should think: *"That's a proper job — and I could just message them a photo."*

## Video (optional, later)
A short 5–10s clean process/reveal clip (before → repair → after) performs very well in Reels/Stories. When Josh supplies a video, we build a 9:16 reveal ad alongside the static before/afters.
