# Image Specifications — SMART Repairs

## Aspect ratios & where they perform
| Ratio | Dimensions | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **4:5** (vertical) | **1080 × 1350** | **Facebook & Instagram Feed** — takes the most screen on mobile, highest feed CTR. **Primary format.** |
| **1:1** (square) | **1080 × 1080** | Safe all-rounder for Feed and some placements; use if you can't make 4:5. |
| **9:16** (full vertical) | **1080 × 1920** | **Stories & Reels** — full-screen. Provide this so Advantage+ placements deliver well beyond the feed. |

## Recommendation per ad
For **each** of the 4 ads, ideally supply **two** versions:
1. **4:5 (1080×1350)** — the workhorse for feed.
2. **9:16 (1080×1920)** — for Stories/Reels.

If you only have time for one per ad, make it **4:5** — Meta will auto-crop it into other placements (just check the crops look OK). Two native ratios always beat one auto-cropped one.

## Safe zones (so nothing important gets cut or covered)
- Keep the **repaired area, the car, and any "Before/After" label within the central ~80%** of the frame.
- **9:16 (Stories/Reels):** leave the **top ~14%** and **bottom ~20%** clear of key content — that's where the profile name, caption and CTA button sit. Don't put the "after" or a label down there.
- **4:5:** keep text/label clear of the very bottom (headline/CTA render below the image).

## Cropping & framing
- Same **angle, distance and lighting** for before and after — the honest transformation is the whole point.
- Fill the frame with the car/panel; avoid distracting backgrounds (bins, clutter, number plates — blur/obscure plates).
- Landscape (16:9 / horizontal) shots are the **worst** for mobile — always shoot/crop **vertical or square**.

## Technical
- **Format:** JPG or PNG, high quality (not blurry/compressed).
- **Min resolution:** 1080px on the shortest side.
- **File:** clean, sharp, well-lit. A great before/after out-performs any amount of graphic design.

## Important (learned the hard way)
When it's time to publish, the most reliable way to get an image live via the MCP is a **direct public image URL** (e.g. postimages.org). If a media-library upload won't attach, host the image and send the link — it always works.
