# Scaling Strategy — SMART Repairs (£5 → £10 → £20 → £50/day)

The enemy of scaling is **resetting the learning phase**. Big sudden budget jumps spook Meta's delivery and can spike your cost per conversation. Two safe methods:

## Method A — Gradual increases (simplest, recommended to start)
- Increase the daily budget by **no more than ~20–30% at a time**.
- Wait **3–4 days** between increases so delivery re-stabilises.
- Only increase when the last few days' **cost per conversation is acceptable and steady**.

**Example ramp (each step held ~3–4 days if healthy):**
- £5 → £6.50 → £8 → **£10** → £13 → £16 → **£20** → £25 → £32 → £40 → **£50**
- Rough real-world pace: **£5 to ~£20/day over ~2–3 weeks**, then on toward £50 as bookings justify it.
- If a jump spikes cost per conversation, **step back down** one level and hold.

## Method B — Duplicate to jump (for bigger leaps like £20 → £50)
Instead of shocking the live ad set, **duplicate the winning ad set** at the higher budget and run it alongside. The duplicate learns fresh but from a proven creative/audience, so it ramps fast. Once it's performing, you can pause the smaller original. This is the cleaner way to make **larger** jumps without disturbing what's working.

## Capacity check (do this before every increase)
SMART repairs are **you doing the work** — scale spend only as fast as you can **deliver the jobs**. More budget = more enquiries = more diary pressure. If WhatsApp is already full, hold the budget and clear the backlog first (better to keep response times fast than to drown).

## Bid strategy as you scale
- Start on **Highest volume (lowest cost)**.
- Once you know your acceptable cost per conversation / cost per booked job, you *can* add a **cost cap** to protect efficiency at higher budgets — but only after you have solid data. Don't cap too early or it'll throttle delivery.

## Seasonality
Keep an eye on demand — kerbing/scuffs are year-round, but you may see lifts around weather/holiday periods. Scale into strength.

## The one-line rule
**Scale on booked jobs, in ~20–30% steps every few days — or duplicate the winner for bigger jumps — and never faster than you can deliver.**
