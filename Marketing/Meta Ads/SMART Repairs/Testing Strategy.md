# Testing Strategy — SMART Repairs

## Golden rule
**Leave it alone.** Every edit (budget, audience, creative, on/off) restarts Meta's learning and wastes spend. Once live, **do not touch it for at least the first 5–7 days.**

## Learning phase
- Meta wants roughly **~50 optimisation events (conversations) in ~7 days** to exit "Learning." At a low local budget you may sit in "Learning Limited" — that's OK; judge by **cost per conversation and booked jobs**, not the label.
- Don't panic on day 1–2. Delivery is uneven while it learns.

## Metrics that matter (in order)
1. **Booked jobs** (your #1 — tag them in WhatsApp).
2. **Cost per conversation started** (Meta's headline number for this objective).
3. **Conversations started** (volume).
4. **Photos received / qualified enquiries** (quality of the conversations).
5. **CTR** (creative strength), **CPM** (auction cost), **Frequency** (fatigue).

## When to KILL an ad
- After a fair sample (guide: **~£15–£20 spent OR ~1,000+ impressions** on that ad, once the campaign is a few days in), if it has **0 conversations** *and* a clearly lower CTR than the others → pause it.
- After **7 days**, pause the worst performer on **cost per conversation** and let budget flow to the winners.
- Don't kill on impressions Meta never gave an ad — if Meta starved it (tiny spend), that's Meta's verdict; leave it.

## When to DUPLICATE
- When one ad is a **clear winner** (lowest cost per conversation, steady), **duplicate it** and test a **new image or a new copy angle** against it. Winners breed the next test.

## When to INCREASE budget (see Scaling Strategy for the numbers)
- Only once it's **out of/settled in learning** and cost per conversation is **acceptable and stable for ~3–4 days**.

## Warning thresholds (local Warwickshire context)
- **Frequency:** creeping above **~2.5–3.0 over 7 days** → audience fatigue; refresh creative or widen the area slightly.
- **CTR:** below **~1%** (all) is weak creative — expect **2%+** from good before/afters. A falling CTR over time = fatigue.
- **CPC / CPM:** watch the **trend**, not the absolute. Rising CPM + rising cost-per-conversation + flat conversations = fatigue → refresh creative (don't just throw budget at it).
- **Cost per conversation drifting up** week on week with no new creative = time to refresh images.

## Cadence
- **Days 1–5:** hands off. Just make sure it's delivering and replying fast on WhatsApp.
- **Day 7:** first proper review — pause the clear loser, note the winner.
- **Weekly thereafter:** review the checklist (`Performance Checklist.md`), refresh a creative if fatiguing, scale if healthy.
