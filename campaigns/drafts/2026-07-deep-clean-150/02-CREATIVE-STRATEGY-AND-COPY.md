# Creative Strategy & Copy Bank — Deep Clean £150

## 1. The creative thesis

**The transformation sells the service. The offer only removes hesitation.**

Every ad follows the same architecture:

1. **A specific, private observation** that makes someone feel seen ("you stopped offering lifts", "you stop noticing it, your passengers don't")
2. **Proof** — the before/after does the persuading, not adjectives
3. **Mechanism** — *why* it works (shampooed and extracted, A/C steam-sanitised, iron fallout removed). This is what separates £150 from £40.
4. **Convenience** — we come to you, across Warwickshire
5. **Offer, last and quiet** — "Usually £180. Currently £150."

The offer is the **final line**, never the hook. The moment price leads, Blue Horizon becomes a cheap valet with a discount, and the knowledge file's core positioning rule is broken.

## 2. Voice rules applied

**Doing:** calm, specific, confident, British English, concrete nouns, service mechanisms named exactly as the knowledge file describes them.

**Not doing:** no emojis, no exclamation marks, no "HURRY", no "TODAY ONLY", no fake countdowns, no "miracle", no "showroom finish guaranteed", no red price stickers, no capitals-shouting. The word "cheap" never appears except when describing what Blue Horizon is *not*.

**Price presentation, always:**
> Usually £180. Currently £150.

Never "£30 OFF!", never "HALF PRICE", never a slashed-through number in a starburst.

# 6. Visual & format specification

| Ratio | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **4:5** | 1080 × 1350 | **Primary.** Facebook & Instagram feed. |
| **9:16** | 1080 × 1920 | Stories & Reels — required for Advantage+ placements to deliver well. |
| 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | Fallback only. |

**Before/after presentation rules:**
- Identical angle, distance and lighting in both halves — the honesty is the entire persuasive mechanism.
- Vertical stack (before above, after below) reads better on mobile than side-by-side, which shrinks both halves.
- Keep the transformation inside the central 80% of the frame.
- 9:16: leave the top ~14% and bottom ~20% clear — that's where the profile name and CTA button sit.
- Obscure number plates.
- No graphic overlays, no starbursts, no price stickers, no arrows, no "BEFORE"/"AFTER" in a loud font. If a label is needed, small and understated. **The image should look like a photograph, not a poster.**

**Reference standard:** Porsche and Gtechniq marketing. Restraint, negative space, real photography, no visual shouting.
