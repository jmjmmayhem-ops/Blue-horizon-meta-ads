## Two findings that fall straight out of this

**1. Adding headlights to a valet takes it from £256/day to £391/day.** Same driveway, one extra hour, £90. That single bundle turns your weakest service into a near-target one. It should be offered on *every* valet booking, in the ad and in the WhatsApp reply.

**2. SMART repairs hit £400/day on their own** — and there are **four competing ads in the entire UK**.
