## Structure (same proven pattern as July/August)
- One campaign per service, objective Engagement → optimisation **Conversations** → destination **WhatsApp** (07818 514079), Page 110694795276891.
- Creatives built inline with `call_to_action.type = WHATSAPP_MESSAGE` and `app_destination = WHATSAPP` so Meta attributes conversations natively (the fix from 29 Jul).
- Service-specific prefilled first message in the link `?text=`, plain text, so Josh gets car + postcode in the first message.
- Campaign budget optimisation, daily budgets set as *proposals* (total £72/day if everything were on; see recommendation).
- Targeting: **broad** = Rugby +20 mi, ages 25–65 (Advantage+ audience on). **high-value** = Rugby +25 mi, ages 30–65 **hard cap**, Advantage+ audience off (fixes the 24% age leak found in the 26 Aug review).
- Images: 25 uploaded from the live website (public /images URLs) plus the proven summer before/afters already in the account. Every website image is a real Blue Horizon job.

## Recommendation (APPROVE / REJECT / EDIT)

## How to publish
1. Ads Manager → the green **Review and publish** button (top right) lists the 13 draft campaigns, 13 ad sets, 32 ads. Publish.
2. Everything publishes **paused**. Toggle on only the campaigns approved above.
3. Tap one live ad from a phone: the WhatsApp prefill must appear with normal spaces (no %20).
4. Within 24 h check Meta reports conversations > 0 on a delivering ad. If it stays at zero with messages arriving, the WhatsApp Business connection in Business Settings is the suspect, not the creative.
