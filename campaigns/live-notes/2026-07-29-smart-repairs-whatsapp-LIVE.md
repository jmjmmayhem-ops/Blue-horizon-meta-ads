## What the test actually is
Copy + headline only. Every ad carries the same Send Message button, because a WhatsApp Messages campaign permits no other CTA (Meta rejects others at delivery — this cost us a rebuild on 29 Jul). The A/B is *message angle*, not button.

## Watch-outs for the first week
- **Hands off days 1–5** (see `Testing Strategy.md`). Every edit restarts learning.
- **8 ads on £10/day is a wide spread** — Meta will concentrate on 2–3 winners and starve the rest. Expected; don't read low impressions on an ad as a verdict Meta never tested.
- **Reply speed on WhatsApp is the single biggest driver of bookings.** Minutes, not hours.
- **Tag every conversation:** photo received / quoted / booked / out of area / not proceeding.
- Rear bumper 2 image is 744×552 (below ideal, will auto-crop). Door scratch is the best image of the set at 1200×1600.

## Day 7 review
Pause the clear loser on cost per conversation, note the winner, and only then consider scaling (`Scaling Strategy.md` — ~20–30% steps). Judge on **booked jobs**, not message volume.
