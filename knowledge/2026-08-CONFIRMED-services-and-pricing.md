# WHAT THIS CHANGES ABOUT THE ADS

**1. New Car Protection was badly undersold.** The live ads say "ceramic coating with hydrophobic protection." Reality is **5-year paint coating + glass coating + alloy coating + marring removal + warranty**. Competitors advertise coating-only at £399–£600. Blue Horizon isn't the expensive option — it's the only complete one. **"Three coatings, not one."** Rebuild required.

**2. Durability claims are now available.** Previously avoided for lack of evidence. The site states 5 years with a warranty — that matches or beats every competitor claim found in the ad library.

**3. Sealant vs ceramic is the honest upsell line.** Enhancement and Refined finish in sealant; Flawless and NCP in ceramic with warranty. That's the real reason to move someone from £595 to £745.

**4. Headlight restoration is the most under-used margin in the business.** £90 on a visit already booked. Valet alone ≈ £240/day; valet + headlights ≈ £340/day for one extra hour.

**5. Real named reviews should be in the ads.** Competitors headline review counts. Blue Horizon has six strong, specific, public testimonials and uses none of them.

**6. "We bring the water, you provide a socket"** removes the top practical objection and appears in no current ad.
