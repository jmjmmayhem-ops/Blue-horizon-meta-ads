# PART 1 — The single biggest finding

I searched the UK ad library by category. The competition is wildly uneven:

| Category | Active UK ads |
|---|---|
| Car detailing (general) | **3,139** |
| Ceramic coating / paint correction | **192** |
| **Mobile SMART / bumper scuff repair** | **4** |

**Four.** Nationally. And SMART repairs earn Josh **~£430 per working day** — second only to Enhancement Detail.

The three advertisers in that space (ChipsAway, The SMART Repair People) all run the identical hook: **"Get Your FREE Estimate" / "Get Your Free Car Repair Quote Today."** Photo-based quoting is the category norm, which maps perfectly onto WhatsApp.

**This is the most under-exploited opportunity in the entire business.** High yield per day, almost no competition, and a quoting mechanism Blue Horizon already has working.

## 🔴 Discrepancies — these need answers before I build ads

**Q1. "Daily Defender" and "The Collector's Package."** Both appear as detailing bundles on the site. Neither exists in the knowledge file. What are they, what's included, what do they cost, and should they be advertised?

**Q2. "Prices start from £350."** One detailing page says this; the knowledge file and another page both say Enhancement Detail starts at £495. Which is correct? If there's a £350 entry tier, that changes the competitive picture significantly — the market advertises ceramic at £399–£600, so £350 would put Blue Horizon *at* market rather than above it.

**Q3. "Signature Valet — finished with a 1-year ceramic coating."** The knowledge file says Deep Clean includes ceramic protection lasting **6–8 months**, and explicitly forbids durability claims that aren't product-backed. The site is claiming **1 year**. Which is right, and can I use a duration in ads? *This matters commercially:* competitors advertise "3-Year" and "5-Year" protection. If Gtechniq CSL supports a stated figure, not using it is costing bookings.

**Q4. "Platinum Valet" / "Platinum Deep Clean" / "Signature Valet."** The site uses these names; the knowledge file uses Maintenance Valet / Maintenance Plus / Deep Clean. Are these the same things under different names, or additional tiers? Ads must use whatever the customer sees on the site.

**Q5. Ozone treatment.** Listed on the site, absent from the knowledge file. Price? Time? It's a strong angle for smoke/pet/damp odour — a genuinely differentiated service.

**Q6. Aviation detailing.** The site advertises aircraft valeting for private owners, pilots and airfields — light aircraft, helicopters, turboprops, business jets. **This is completely absent from the knowledge file** and is potentially the highest-value service in the business. What do these jobs pay, how long do they take, how often do you get them, and do you want ads? There is essentially no competition for this on Meta.

**Q7. Alloy wheel refurbishment.** The site sells it. The knowledge file says hold all alloy ads until you explicitly ask. **Do you want alloy ads built?** I haven't drafted any.

**Q8. "Custom Package Builder."** There's a package-builder page. Is that a live quoting tool? If it works well it might be a better ad destination than WhatsApp for the detailing tier.

**Q9. Headlight restoration and soft top** — knowledge file says £90/pair and from £150. Still current?

## 🟠 The URL problem (worth fixing regardless)

Your location pages sit on URLs like:
```
/home-page-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-2-2   → Daventry
/home-page-2-2-2-2-2-2-2       → Coventry
/home-page-2-2-2-2-2-2         → Warwick
```
You've built the pages — the hard part — and given away the SEO value with the URLs. `/mobile-valeting-coventry` would rank. `/home-page-2-2-2-2-2-2-2` won't.

Note some newer areas *do* use good URLs (`/service-areas/coleshill`, `/service-areas/henley-in-arden`), so the fix is already half-done. Worth finishing.

# PART 6 — Three changes to make across every ad

1. **Reviews into headlines.** "4.8★ from 50+ Google Reviews" belongs in the headline, not the description. Every strong competitor does this and Blue Horizon doesn't.
2. **Anchor every high-ticket price.** "Usually £X, currently £Y" works on the Deep Clean; nothing else uses it. The whole market anchors.
3. **Multiple headline variants per ad.** The better competitors run 5–6. Blue Horizon runs one.
