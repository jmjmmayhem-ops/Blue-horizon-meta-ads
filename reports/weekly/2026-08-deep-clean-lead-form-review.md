## Age targeting is not applied as specified

Spec was 30-65. Actual delivery includes:
- 18-24: 2,212 impressions, £8.89
- 65+: 9,441 impressions, £78.32

~24% of spend went outside the intended age band. Either the age cap was not
set at build time, or Advantage+ Audience is on and overriding it.

## Recommendations

1. Do NOT change targeting on this campaign — it is deliberately idling at
   £5/day and a targeting edit resets the learning phase.
2. Apply to the NEXT campaign build: age 25-64, and verify Advantage+ Audience
   is off.
3. AD4 (Not A Car Wash) is the weakest at £5.35 CPL — retire it in the next round.
4. AD2's angle (relief / no judgement) is the copy direction to build on.
